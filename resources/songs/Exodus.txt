Phazite's Exodus example song plays the notes of "Exodus" by XCALIBUR
(https://soundcloud.com/xc4libur/exodus), taken from the LMMS project that
XCALIBUR shared on the LMMS Sharing Platform
(https://lmms.io/lsp/?action=show&file=12900) under the Creative Commons
Attribution license, version 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), whose text follows. The
platform names no version; 4.0 was the current one when the project was
shared, on 18 March 2018.

What Phazite changed: it took the notes alone (resources/songs/Exodus.mid,
written from the project by tools/lmms-to-midi.ps1) and plays them as the
recording does: without the project's bars 24 to 32, its pitched parts a
fourth higher, with the recording's tempo and with the drums in the intro. It
plays them on its own instruments and effects, on presets made after the
original's sounds and mixed as the original is, with synthesized drums in
place of its drum samples. None of the original's sounds or samples are in
Phazite.
