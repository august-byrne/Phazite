## What ships with Phazite

[resources/licenses/notices.json](../resources/licenses/notices.json) is the one list of everything from others compiled into the app and its helper, with each one's version, license, copyright, what Phazite uses it for, and the files its notice is made of. As CMake configures, [cmake/PhaziteNotices.cmake](../cmake/PhaziteNotices.cmake) puts each one's notice into `licenses/` next to the app, with Phazite's own license (`licenses/Phazite.txt`), and writes `NOTICES.txt` beside it, with Phazite's notice, every entry and the trademarks. **Help > About Phazite** lists them all, Phazite's first, with the text of the one chosen; the installer shows `NOTICES.txt` on its first page and installs both. `tests/AboutPanelTests.cpp` checks that every entry has its text there, and that nothing else is.

A new dependency that ships goes into that list, with its notice; one compiled into one platform's builds only says so (`"platforms": [ "windows" ]`, as the ASIO SDK does).
